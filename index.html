01 — ABOUT

Redefining
Possibility

Techfest is the annual science and technology festival of IIT Bombay. Since 1998, it has been a platform where 190,000 participants from across 2,300 colleges and 56 nations converge to push the boundaries of human innovation.

It stands as Asia's largest gathering of scientific minds — a crucible where robotics, artificial intelligence, biotechnology and space exploration collide in spectacular fashion.
