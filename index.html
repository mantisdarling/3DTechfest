SECTION 01 ABOUT

Redefining
Possibility

Techfest is the annual science and technology festival of the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay. Established in 1998 by the student community of IIT Bombay, the festival operates as a non profit student organization.

Over twenty eight editions, Techfest has grown into Asia largest gathering of scientific minds, welcoming more than 180,000 attendees across 2,300 universities and 56 participating nations. It holds the patronage of international bodies including UNESCO and UNICEF.

28 Years of Continuous Innovation

Competitions and Hackathons

Challenging coding marathons, aerial robotics, autonomous navigation systems, and clean energy prototypes tested under rigorous real world benchmarks.

International Robowars

Heavyweight combat robotics championship where remote and autonomous bots clash inside a high velocity reinforced steel arena.

Ideate Social Innovation

Transformative technical solutions engineered for agriculture, biomedical accessibility, and ecological conservation with venture acceleration.
